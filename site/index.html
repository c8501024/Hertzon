  • Microwave circuits: microstrip lines and filters with S-parameters, Smith charts and frequency sweeps
  • Waveguides and coaxial lines: wave ports, modes and field plots

  • Half and quarter models with symmetry planes: the far field includes the mirror images

The vision: co-design

Every design, made by engineer and AI together

Hertzon provides its methods as tools that an AI agent can call through MCP (what is MCP?). The AI picks and combines them; the engineer sets the goal, sees every step in the open window and decides.

Methods, not guesses

The AI does not guess physics. It uses the same validated methods as you: adaptive FEM solving, sweeps, optimizers, derivative tuning, circuits and PCB import. The numbers come from the solver.

You stay in control

Every change appears at once in the open project. You can step in at any time, and Ctrl+Z undoes anything the AI did. What it builds is an ordinary Hertzon project.

Next: topology optimization

From the author's research on pixelated antennas (Sci. Rep. 2024): the shape of the metal itself is optimized, so the AI can explore designs that no parametric model contains.

One sentence to the AI, and Hertzon builds the model

Hertzon's MCP server lets an AI assistant draw the geometry, assign ports and boundaries, start the solver, read the results and decide what to try next, all in the open Hertzon window. These are replays of real runs: the times, run counts and results at the end are what came out.

Next: fewer tokens, so that small models can do it too

In the glasses benchmark the AI spent most of its effort steering parametric sweeps by hand. The patch tool shows the other way: the AI states the goal, and Hertzon's optimizer does the iterations in one call.

Optimizers for every structure

The efficient optimizer behind the patch antenna tool (a derivative trust region on the adaptive mesh, matched in two evaluations above) is being integrated for any geometry with design variables.

Goals instead of loops

The AI describes the target (bands, return loss, efficiency) and gets back a finished, verified design, instead of reading and comparing dozens of runs itself.

For small local models

With the iterations inside Hertzon, each step needs only a short tool call and a short answer. The aim is that small models running on your own computer can drive Hertzon as well.

Why Hertzon

A classic engineering workflow, open all the way down

A desktop application (Qt + VTK) that stays responsive with large models, for Windows and Linux.

Adaptive meshing

The FEM frequency-domain solver refines the mesh until the S-parameters converge. EMerge's AURA refinement, extended with hzAURA and curved elements, or Hertzon's own Delaunay mesher.

Sweeps and optimization

Fast and interpolating frequency sweeps, parallel sweep points, parametric sweeps and an optimizer for design variables.

Derivative tuning

Sliders for design variables: S-parameters, Smith charts and radiation patterns follow live without solving again.

Circuits and 3D

Schematics with lines, lumped parts and Touchstone blocks, analyzed in a moment, and turned into a 3D layout that is solved full-wave.

Solve anywhere

The solver runs in its own process, on this computer, another one in the network, or a cloud server created for the session (experimental).

AI assistants and Python

An MCP server lets AI assistants build, solve and tune models in the open window. Python scripting for everything else.
